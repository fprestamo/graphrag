Elon Musk, the billionaire entrepreneur, is the CEO of Tesla, Inc. and the founder of SpaceX.
He was born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa.
Musk co-founded PayPal and later founded The Boring Company.
Under his leadership, Tesla became the world's most valuable automaker.
SpaceX, officially known as Space Exploration Technologies Corp, launched the Falcon 9 rocket
and became the first private company to dock a spacecraft with the International Space Station.

Jeff Bezos founded Amazon in 1994 out of his garage in Bellevue, Washington.
Amazon started as an online bookstore and grew into the world's largest e-commerce platform.
Bezos also founded Blue Origin, a private aerospace company focused on reusable rockets.
He stepped down as Amazon CEO in 2021, transitioning to Executive Chairman.

Apple Inc. was co-founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in 1976.
The company is headquartered in Cupertino, California.
Apple is known for the iPhone, iPad, MacBook, and the Apple Watch.
Tim Cook became CEO of Apple after Steve Jobs passed away in 2011.

Microsoft Corporation was founded by Bill Gates and Paul Allen in 1975.
The company is headquartered in Redmond, Washington.
Microsoft produces the Windows operating system and the Office productivity suite.
In 2014, Satya Nadella became CEO of Microsoft, leading a major cloud transformation.

Google was founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin in 1998 while they were PhD students at Stanford.
The company is now a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., which was created in 2015 as a holding company.
Google is the world's most widely used search engine and also develops Android.
Sundar Pichai serves as CEO of both Google and Alphabet.
