The World Health Organization, commonly known as WHO, is a specialized agency of the United Nations.
It was established in 1948 and is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.
WHO coordinates international public health responses, including the COVID-19 pandemic response.
Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus has served as Director-General of the WHO since 2017.

The United Nations (UN) is an international intergovernmental organization founded in 1945.
It replaced the League of Nations after World War II.
The UN has 193 member states and six principal organs: the General Assembly, Security Council,
Secretariat, International Court of Justice, Trusteeship Council, and Economic and Social Council.
António Guterres serves as the Secretary-General of the United Nations.

The International Monetary Fund, known as the IMF, is a Washington D.C.-based financial organization.
It was established in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference alongside the World Bank.
The IMF monitors global economic trends and provides financial assistance to member countries.
Kristalina Georgieva is the Managing Director of the International Monetary Fund.

The North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) is a military alliance founded in 1949.
It was established as a collective defense agreement among Western democracies.
NATO has 31 member states as of 2023. Its headquarters are in Brussels, Belgium.
Jens Stoltenberg served as Secretary General of NATO from 2014 until 2023.

The European Union (EU) is a political and economic union of 27 member states in Europe.
It was formally established by the Maastricht Treaty in 1993.
The EU operates a single market and its own currency, the Euro, used by 20 member countries.
Ursula von der Leyen is the President of the European Commission.

The World Trade Organization, or WTO, is an intergovernmental organization regulating trade.
It replaced the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT) in 1995.
The WTO provides a framework for negotiating and formalizing trade agreements.
Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala serves as Director-General of the World Trade Organization.
