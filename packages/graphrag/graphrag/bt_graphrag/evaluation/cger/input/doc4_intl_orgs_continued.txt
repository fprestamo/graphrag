The World Health Organisation (WHO) responded to the coronavirus pandemic with global guidelines.
Its mandate includes disease prevention, health policy, and pharmaceutical standard-setting.
The WHO works with governments to strengthen health systems and eradicate infectious diseases.
Member states contribute to WHO's budget through assessed and voluntary contributions.

The United Nations Organization, founded after WWII, promotes international peace and security.
The UN Security Council has five permanent members: the USA, China, Russia, France, and the UK.
The UN General Assembly meets annually and each member state has one vote.
The UN Secretariat, led by the Secretary-General, manages the organization's day-to-day work.

International Monetary Fund (IMF) provides balance-of-payments support to struggling economies.
The fund intervened during the 2008 financial crisis and the European sovereign debt crisis.
IMF's Special Drawing Rights (SDR) serve as an international reserve asset.
Its governance structure gives larger economies more voting power.

NATO's founding treaty, signed in Washington D.C. in 1949, commits members to collective defense.
The alliance coordinates military exercises and intelligence sharing among member nations.
NATO expanded eastward after the Cold War, admitting former Warsaw Pact countries.
In 2022, Finland and Sweden applied for NATO membership following Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

The European Union's single market allows free movement of goods, services, capital, and people.
The EU Parliament is elected directly by citizens of member states every five years.
The European Central Bank manages monetary policy for the Eurozone countries.
Brexit in 2020 saw the United Kingdom leave the European Union.

The WTO dispute settlement mechanism resolves trade conflicts between member nations.
Developing nations receive special and differential treatment under WTO agreements.
The WTO's Trade Facilitation Agreement entered into force in 2017.
Trade negotiations under the WTO are conducted in rounds, such as the Doha Development Round.
