Elon Reeve Musk is a technology entrepreneur and investor. He heads Tesla Motors and runs
Space Exploration Technologies. Musk acquired Twitter in 2022 and rebranded it as X.
He was also an early investor in OpenAI, an artificial intelligence research organization.

Jeffrey Preston Bezos, commonly known as Jeff Bezos, is an American entrepreneur.
He is the founder and former chief executive of Amazon.com, the global e-commerce giant.
Bezos launched Blue Origin, his aerospace venture, with the goal of reducing space travel costs.
In 2023, he became one of the world's wealthiest individuals, surpassing Elon Musk on some lists.

Apple Incorporated is an American multinational technology company based in Cupertino.
Its flagship product is the iPhone, introduced in 2007, which revolutionized the smartphone industry.
The company also sells the Mac line of computers, AirPods, and the Apple TV streaming device.
Apple's App Store hosts over two million applications and generates billions in annual revenue.

Microsoft Corp. is an American technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington.
It develops the Azure cloud platform, which competes with Amazon Web Services.
Microsoft acquired LinkedIn in 2016 and GitHub in 2018.
Satya Nadella, who became Microsoft's chief executive officer in 2014, led the push into cloud services.

Alphabet Incorporated is the parent company of Google and several other subsidiaries.
Google Search remains the dominant web search engine, handling billions of queries each day.
Google also operates YouTube, Google Maps, and the Google Play app marketplace.
Larry Page and Sergey Brin, the co-founders of Google, serve as board members of Alphabet.

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence company founded in 2015. It is headquartered in San Francisco.
The organization created ChatGPT, GPT-4, and the DALL-E image generation system.
Sam Altman is the CEO of OpenAI. The company received major investments from Microsoft.
