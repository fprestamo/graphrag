Elon Musk runs Tesla as its top executive. He is the principal decision-maker at the automaker.
Musk manages SpaceX in his role as its chief. He directs the company's mission to Mars.
Jeff Bezos established Amazon and was in charge of the company for nearly three decades.
Andy Jassy took over Amazon's top role from Bezos. He now directs the e-commerce and cloud giant.

Bill Gates started Microsoft alongside Paul Allen. Gates was at the helm of Microsoft until 2000.
Steve Jobs helmed Apple from its founding through 1985, and again from 1997 until his death in 2011.
Tim Cook took the reins at Apple following Jobs' passing and continues to steer the company.
Satya Nadella took charge of Microsoft in 2014, transforming it into a cloud-first organization.

OpenAI was established in 2015 by a group of tech entrepreneurs including Sam Altman and Elon Musk.
Sam Altman oversees OpenAI in his capacity as its CEO and guides its research agenda.
Ilya Sutskever co-founded OpenAI and served as its chief scientist before departing in 2024.
Microsoft has invested heavily in OpenAI, making it one of the company's strategic partners.

NVIDIA was set up by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem in Santa Clara in 1993.
Huang is at the top of NVIDIA and steers its strategy in AI chips and graphics technology.
AMD competes with NVIDIA in the graphics and data center chip market.
Intel Corporation is a semiconductor manufacturer that rivals both AMD and NVIDIA.

Google was established by Larry Page and Sergey Brin in a garage in Menlo Park, California.
Sundar Pichai is in charge of Google and also commands Alphabet, its parent holding company.
YouTube is owned by Google. It was acquired in 2006 and remains under Google's control.
The Android mobile operating system is developed and maintained by Google.

Meta was previously called Facebook before being rebranded in October 2021.
Mark Zuckerberg heads Meta and determines the company's direction in social media and the metaverse.
Instagram and WhatsApp are owned by Meta. Both were acquired by Facebook before the rebrand.
Meta develops virtual reality hardware through its Reality Labs division.
