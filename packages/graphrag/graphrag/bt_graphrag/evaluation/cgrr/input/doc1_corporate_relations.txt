Elon Musk is the chief executive officer of Tesla, Inc. He leads the company's strategic direction.
Musk heads SpaceX as its founder and CEO. He runs daily operations at the aerospace firm.
Bill Gates co-founded Microsoft Corporation together with Paul Allen. Gates serves on its board.
Satya Nadella directs Microsoft as its chief executive. He manages the company's cloud strategy.
Tim Cook is the CEO of Apple Inc. He oversees Apple's global operations and product lineup.
Larry Page co-founded Google with Sergey Brin. He acted as Google's chief executive until 2001.

Jeff Bezos founded Amazon.com in 1994 and served as its chief executive for 27 years.
Andy Jassy succeeded Jeff Bezos as Amazon's CEO in 2021. He was previously head of AWS.
Sam Altman leads OpenAI as its chief executive officer. He was previously president of Y Combinator.
Jensen Huang is the founder and CEO of NVIDIA Corporation. He co-founded the company in 1993.
Mark Zuckerberg is the co-founder and chief executive of Meta Platforms, formerly known as Facebook.
Sundar Pichai heads both Google and Alphabet Inc. as their chief executive officer.

Apple Inc. produces the iPhone, a line of smartphones. The iPhone was introduced by Steve Jobs in 2007.
Tesla manufactures electric vehicles including the Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y.
Microsoft develops the Windows operating system and the Azure cloud computing platform.
Amazon operates AWS, the world's leading cloud infrastructure provider.
NVIDIA manufactures graphics processing units widely used in gaming and AI workloads.
Google operates the world's most popular search engine and the Chrome web browser.

SpaceX launched the Falcon 9 reusable rocket. The company also develops the Starship spacecraft.
Blue Origin, founded by Jeff Bezos, develops the New Shepard suborbital rocket.
Boeing manufactures commercial aircraft, including the 737 and 787 Dreamliner series.
Airbus, headquartered in Toulouse, France, produces the A320 and A380 aircraft families.

Tesla acquired SolarCity in 2016. The merger was approved by Tesla's shareholders.
Microsoft purchased LinkedIn in 2016 for approximately 26 billion US dollars.
Amazon bought Whole Foods Market in 2017. The deal was worth around 13.7 billion dollars.
Google acquired YouTube in 2006 for 1.65 billion dollars in stock.
Facebook purchased Instagram in 2012 and WhatsApp in 2014 for significant sums.
