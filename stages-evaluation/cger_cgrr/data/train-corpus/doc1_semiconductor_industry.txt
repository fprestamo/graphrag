Semiconductor Industry Leaders in 2024

Jensen Huang co-founded NVIDIA Corporation in 1993 and has served as its chief executive ever since. Mr. Huang heads NVIDIA, which is headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Under J. Huang, NVIDIA Corp. has grown to become the dominant supplier of GPUs for AI workloads.

Lisa Su is the chair and CEO of Advanced Micro Devices, better known as AMD. Dr. Su was appointed chief executive of AMD in October 2014. She also sits on the board of Cisco Systems. AMD acquired Xilinx in 2022 for roughly $49 billion.

Pat Gelsinger returned to Intel as chief executive officer in February 2021, after years at VMware. Mr. Gelsinger leads Intel Corporation, which is headquartered in Santa Clara. Intel was founded in 1968 by Gordon Moore and Robert Noyce; Moore famously co-founded Intel Corporation after leaving Fairchild Semiconductor.

TSMC, also known as Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, was established in 1987 by Morris Chang. T.S.M.C. is the world's largest contract chip manufacturer. C. C. Wei is the chief executive of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company and previously served as its chief operating officer. Chang founded TSMC after a long career at Texas Instruments.
