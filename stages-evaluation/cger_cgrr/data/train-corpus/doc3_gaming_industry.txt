The Video-Game Industry After the Microsoft–Activision Deal

In October 2023, Microsoft Corporation completed its acquisition of Activision Blizzard for $68.7 billion. Microsoft Corp. purchased Activision Blizzard, Inc. after a lengthy regulatory review. Phil Spencer is the chief executive officer of Microsoft Gaming, the division of Microsoft that absorbed Activision Blizzard. P. Spencer leads Microsoft Gaming and previously headed Xbox.

Activision Blizzard was formed in 2008 by the merger of Activision, Inc. and Vivendi Games. Bobby Kotick co-founded Activision in 1979 and served as CEO of Activision Blizzard for over three decades. Mr. Kotick resigned from Activision Blizzard, Inc. in late 2023 following the Microsoft acquisition.

Sony Interactive Entertainment, often abbreviated as SIE, is the gaming subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation. SIE was established in 1993 and operates the PlayStation brand. Hideaki Nishino and Hermen Hulst are co-CEOs of Sony Interactive Entertainment. Sony Group Corp. is led by Kenichiro Yoshida, who has been chief executive of Sony since 2018.

Nintendo Co., Ltd., commonly referred to simply as Nintendo, was founded in 1889 by Fusajiro Yamauchi. Shuntaro Furukawa is the president of Nintendo and has run Nintendo Co. since 2018. Mr. Furukawa succeeded Tatsumi Kimishima, who previously led Nintendo Co., Ltd. as president from 2015 to 2018.

Electronic Arts, also known as EA, was co-founded in 1982 by Trip Hawkins. Andrew Wilson is the chief executive of Electronic Arts and has led EA since 2013. E.A. acquired Respawn Entertainment in 2017 and purchased Codemasters in 2021.
